# Rubric, stage 1 (20 points)

The design review: [contract/design-review.md](../../contract/design-review.md), ten rows of 0 to 2, scored by
the grader from the pitch, the design doc, the contract, the operations and the review with its answers.

| Criterion | Points | Full marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rows 1 to 10 of the design review | 20 | Each row 2: sound, and argued where the design lint or a reviewer disagreed |

Pass: 14/20 with no row at 0. A brief of the learner's own is approved here: the pitch must show a product that
can meet every requirement without padding (a union added only to have a union scores row 5 at 0).
