# Hints: 17.10 spec scavenger hunt

1. The spec's table of contents names every section, but not every algorithm: the algorithms are inside the
   sections, written as `Name(args)` followed by numbered steps. Use the browser's find on the full page, with the
   algorithm's name and its opening parenthesis.
2. Several answers are in the grammar productions and their notes, not the prose: escapes, ignored tokens and
   integer ranges are where the lexical grammar and the type `Int` are defined. The built-in directives are
   written out in SDL in section 3.13; read their definitions, defaults included.
3. Questions 9 and 11 are about execution order and subscriptions: read the operation types in 6.2 and the
   subscription rule in 5.2.4, and look for the exact words the questions quote.
