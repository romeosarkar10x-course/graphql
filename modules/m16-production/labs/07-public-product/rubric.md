# Rubric: 16.7 public product (CHANGELOG, MIGRATION, ANNOUNCEMENT, PEER-REVIEW)

Pass mark: 6 of 10.

| Points | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | The announcement leads with what changes and who is affected; a reader can decide in ten seconds |
| 2 | Every date is concrete, and what happens after the support date is stated |
| 2 | Each breaking change has a replacement and a working "after" operation in the guide |
| 2 | Claims about who is affected are backed by usage data or are stated as limits of it |
| 2 | The peer review records concrete findings and the changes they caused |
