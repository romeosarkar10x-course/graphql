# Lab 7.1 notes

## 1. Screens first

For each screen, list what it needs that the starter draft could not give it (at least three per screen).

## 2. Decisions

Three design decisions you made (a name, a nullability choice, an argument) and the screen need behind each.

## 3. The SDL-only test

Give your `shelfie.graphqls` (nothing else: no screens, no README) to a teammate, or solo: to yourself a
day later, or to an AI assistant with "build the club page from this schema". Write down every question
they ask before they can build it. How many? Which ones would a description have answered?

## 4. One change after the test

What you changed in the schema because of the questions.
