# Hall of shame

## 1. Rank the cards

Worst first. Score each 1 to 5 on **client cost** (how much harder it makes every client's life) and
**lock-in** (how hard it is to fix now), and say whether the case for it convinces you.

| Rank | Card | Client cost | Lock-in | Case for convincing? | One-line verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 2. Fix your worst three

For each: the replacement SDL (valid on its own, check it with `../../../bin/course design lint <file>`),
and the migration: what you add, what you deprecate, and how long the old shape stays.

## 3. Your vote (group) or your pitch (solo)

The one card you'd put on the wall, and the 60-second case you'd make for it.
