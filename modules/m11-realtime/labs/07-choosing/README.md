# Lab 11.7: Choosing the right tool

Lecture: [11.7 Choosing the right tool](../../lectures/11.7-choosing.md). Grading: rubric. Needs nothing.

Fill in `matrix.md`: for six Shelfie features, the delivery mechanism you would choose (query, polling,
subscription, `@defer`, live query, webhook, CDC feeding one of them), the runner-up, and why, using
numbers from your own lab 11.5 and 11.6 runs. Then write `pitch.md`: defend one row in two minutes
(about 250 words) to someone who prefers your runner-up.

**Group variant:** each person pitches a different row for two minutes; the others play the sceptic for one
minute each, then vote on whether the choice stands. Record the objection that came closest in `pitch.md`.

## Done when

`matrix.md` and `pitch.md` pass the rubric (`bin/course grade m11/07`).
